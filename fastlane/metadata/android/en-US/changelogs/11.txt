Crash reporting is now opt-in, and off unless you turn it on.

Earlier versions sent a crash report automatically. They no longer do. The app
asks once, on first run, and you can change the answer any time in
Settings > Support. Reports become issues on a public GitHub tracker, so the
prompt says exactly what a report carries — a stack trace, your device and app
version, and a random ID that links your own reports. It never carries the
system log, your notifications, or usage or health data.
